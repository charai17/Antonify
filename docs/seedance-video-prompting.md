# Seedance-Style Video Prompt Logic

Seedance-style prompts work best when they describe what can be seen or heard, not abstract marketing intent.

## Prompt Shape

Use this order for video prompt fields such as `modelReadyPrompt` or `promptText`:

1. Technical/style capsule
2. Main subject or product
3. Action sequence
4. Environment, set, props, and lighting
5. Camera language
6. Visual style, color palette, texture, or rendering mode
7. Dialogue, voiceover, music, ambience, or sound effects
8. Negative constraints

## Technical Capsule

Use a compact opening when useful:

```text
9:16 vertical ad, 24fps, 6 seconds, clean commercial product lighting.
```

Other examples:

```text
16:9 cinematic product demo, natural daylight, handheld documentary feel.
9:16 UGC-style social ad, phone-camera realism, warm indoor lighting.
9:16 vertical product demo, crisp screen-recording and hand close-up style.
```

## Camera Language

Use concrete camera terms:

- wide shot
- full shot
- medium shot
- close-up
- extreme close-up
- low angle
- high angle
- overhead
- first-person point of view
- tracking shot
- handheld follow
- slow push-in
- pull-back
- pan
- tilt
- orbit
- focus pull
- shallow depth of field
- deep focus
- quick cut feel
- slow motion
- time-lapse
- match cut
- seamless one-take movement

## Product And Ad Actions

Use only when appropriate:

- product close-up
- hands-on use
- product reveal
- practical demonstration
- before/after situation
- first-person usage moment
- 360-degree product rotation
- clean pack shot
- ingredient/material/detail close-up
- simple 3D exploded view

## Reference Assets

Do not invent reference handles.

If the user or app provides reference assets, state the purpose:

```text
Use @image1 for product appearance and material texture.
Use @video1 for camera movement and pacing.
Use @audio1 for music rhythm and ambience.
```

## Negative Constraints

Add constraints to reduce common artifacts:

```text
No extra captions, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

For proof or social proof:

```text
No fake numbers, no fake reviews, no fake logos, no watermarks.
```

## Avoid

Avoid abstract instructions without visual details:

- make it viral
- make it high-converting
- build trust
- increase urgency
- make viewers care
- show premium positioning
- create emotional resonance

Translate those into visible or audible elements.
